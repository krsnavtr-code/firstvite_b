What You Will Actually Do

  • Navigate a data science project using the six phases of the CRISP-DM lifecycle.
  • Write clean Python code utilizing variables, data structures, loops, and functions.
  • Load, inspect, filter, and sort datasets using Pandas DataFrames and NumPy.
  • Master data cleaning techniques: handling missing values and detecting outliers.
  • Conduct complete Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) to extract business insights.
  • Apply fundamental statistics, calculate measures of center/spread, and perform hypothesis testing.
  • Pull and shape data using advanced SQL (Joins, CTEs, and Subqueries).
  • Build KPI reports in Excel using functions like XLOOKUP and Pivot Tables.
  • Execute the ETL process using Power Query and data modeling with DAX in Power BI.
  • Build and present interactive storytelling dashboards using slicers and visuals.
  • Design ML workflows, building Linear Regression, Logistic Regression, and Random Forest models.
  • Execute job-market prep: resume rewriting, LinkedIn optimization, and mock technical interviews.

Do I need coding experience to join?
Not at all. This course is designed for beginners. We teach Python from scratch, right along with all the tools you'll be using. Students with degrees in BBA, BCom, BSc or engineering have all done this and come out the other side.
How does the EMI work?
After the discount of 10% off the fee, which brings it down to ₹44,100, you can split it into 6 monthly payments of ₹7,350 each, no extra costs whatsoever. You pay ₹2,000 upfront to secure your place.
Can I earn through freelancing during the course?
Yes, freelancing is actually part of what we cover on the course. We show you how to find clients on Fiverr and Upwork, what to charge, and how to do a good job. A load of our students have actually earned up to ₹60,000 while still doing the course.
Will AI make data science jobs redundant?
Not quite the opposite actually: it's making the menial bits redundant, but making the job a lot more interesting because people will be needed to design the right questions to ask an AI tool and validate it. That's why we dedicated Module 6 entirely to Generative AI and LLM tools.
Is the certificate recognized by employers?
Yeah, the certificate is actually ISO-certified and issued through our partnership with eklabya, NSDC and NIELIT. That's on top of the project portfolio you'll have built up and your GitHub profile, which is what hiring managers really look at.
How long is the course?
Six months, but don't worry we spread it out with live classes and practical project work along the way. You can choose a weekday or weekend group based on your schedule, and if you miss a session the recording is always there.
Is placement guaranteed?
No program can give you that. What we do promise is that we'll give you 100% support: building your resume, optimizing your LinkedIn, doing mock interviews, and introducing you to our hiring network. 10 students landed jobs in Accenture, IBM, TCS and EY last December thanks to this support.
Are classes online or offline?
They're live online, via Zoom or Google Meet, so you don't need to worry about commuting. And we always record the sessions so you can go back and watch them if you miss one.
What's the real difference between this and free YouTube tutorials?
Well, for a start, if you're paying for a course you get to have some structure, you get some feedback from your tutors, and you've got some accountability, a deadline to finish your project by and all that. You can't get that from just watching free videos.
Who is this programme not a good fit for?
If you're after a course that's just purely self-paced—you know, something you can just do at your own speed with no real live stuff—then this isn't the course for you. This thing is a live, cohort-based course and that's part of what makes it work.
